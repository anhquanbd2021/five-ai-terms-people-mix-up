Term Cost Lab · 5 pairs, 5 simulators

The terms are free.
The confusion is expensive.

Five AI terms people mix up constantly — each pair a simulator where you can pick the wrong side and watch the bill arrive. Fine-tune for facts and get a stale answer; run a loop with no goal and burn the budget; ship a prototype and meet the delivery gates.

Safe educational demo. Everything is a deterministic simulation in your browser — no model is called, nothing is trained, no money is spent. The prices are illustrative; the failure modes are real.
